Curasan Business Intelligence is an internal business reporting and operations application being developed for authorized use by Curasan.

1. Information Processed

The application is designed to process business information from authorized systems, including QuickBooks Online. This may include customer and vendor records, invoices, payments, products, inventory, and accounting transactions.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is intended to support financial reporting, sales analysis, inventory management, business planning, reconciliation, and authorized internal operational workflows.

3. Data Storage

The proposed system uses Python and PostgreSQL for data processing and storage, with Power BI for reporting. The actual production hosting, backup, and storage arrangements must be confirmed before deployment.

4. Access and Security

Access is intended to be limited to authorized personnel. Security controls, credential management, access permissions, and incident response procedures will be established and verified before production use.

5. Third-Party Services

The application is designed to integrate with QuickBooks Online, provided by Intuit. Additional integrations, if enabled, will be documented according to their actual data-sharing practices.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records will be subject to Curasan's approved retention requirements and applicable legal obligations. The retention schedule and deletion procedures must be finalized before production use.

7. Privacy Requests

Questions about access, correction, retention, or deletion of information should be directed to the designated Curasan privacy contact.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, applicable requirements, or approved company practices.
